# Receipt: G13C batch-work 80000 and chunked vocab tail default on M1 Max

Date: 2026-11-26.

## Claim

Two default calibrations for Apple M1 Max (`G13C`, 32 GPU cores):

1. Move `G13C` `batch_work` from `40000` to `80000` (`overlay/mlx/backend/omarchy/encoder.h`). At `40000`, every `Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507-4bit` decode token (`59,920` summed dispatch work-groups) split mid-eval at layer 29 into two submissions (`208 + 53` dispatches, `0.228 ms` inter-submit gap). At `80000`, the whole decode token records into one command buffer and one queue submission (`10.61 ms` decode p50) while keeping all submissions — including `ctx=2048` prefill — under `33.3 ms` (`p99 32.05 ms`, `max 32.09 ms`).
2. Remove the stale `G13C` exclusion from `vocab_tail_chunked_default` (`overlay/mlx/backend/omarchy/device.h`). After single-dispatch online SDPA (#997) brought 4B decode down to `10.9 ms/tok`, the 151,936-column single-workgroup `ArgReduceBF16` (`0.087 ms`) and `LogSumExpBF16` (`0.119 ms`) tail dispatches became a measurable ~1.0% (`~0.11 ms/tok`) cost. Chunking them (`MLX_OMARCHY_ARGREDUCE_CHUNKED` + `MLX_OMARCHY_LSE_CHUNKED`) matches `G14C`/`G15S`/`G16S`/`G17C`/`G18G` (`G13G` stays excluded).
