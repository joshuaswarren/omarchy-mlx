Doctest
  omarchy_sdpa_split_tests (16-inch M1 Max) sha256: 7eeda7b2842d5e5ecc2d611202523565e80a97ed16f302fdf0f31075327ab3a5
  Result: 15 cases, 7 passed, 8 failed; 120238 assertions, 119918 passed, 320 failed. All 320 failures are
  dispatch-count checks (launched == 2 and launched == 2u * rows) that read 0, in old cases and in the new one.
  Every value comparison passed, including the new rows case. Likely cause (inferred from the symbol table, not confirmed):
  the test binary links its own copy of the dispatch counter, and libmlx.so exports none, so the counter the backend
